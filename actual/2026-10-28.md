# Actual, 2026-10-28

## Tap is up for Buffer's app form
tap.bananafest-destiny.com is live from demo/tap: a placeholder home page,
/privacy (what Tap reads, writes, keeps, and how it is deleted) and a
400×400 logo. Both pages carry the pulse tag. / and /logo.png return 200;
/privacy redirects to /privacy/, which returns 200. Gave the boss the values
for Buffer's New Client form.

## Sign in with Buffer, and the first check
The boss created the OAuth client (name Tap, confidential; account:read,
posts:read, posts:write, ideas:read, ideas:write). The client id and secret
are in .git-credentials and set as Worker secrets; a bogus-code call to the
token endpoint gave invalid_grant, so the pair is right. Tap's Worker now has
/auth/start (PKCE S256, state kept in KV for 10 minutes) and /auth/callback.
The callback exchanges the code, keeps the tokens in KV under the Buffer
account id, and runs one check: account, channels, sent posts, post metrics,
read templates and tags, and create-then-delete one template and one tag.
Buffer's auth page accepts the request (303 to its login). Waiting for the
boss to sign in once.

## What the sign-in showed
The boss signed in. Buffer granted account:read, posts:read, posts:write,
ideas:read, ideas:write and offline_access, with a refresh token and a
one-hour access token. The account has one organization and three channels
(Instagram, TikTok, YouTube); five sent posts read back, each with its link.
Reading templates and tags worked, and so did creating and deleting a
template and a tag, so Keep this and Post it again can use real Buffer
templates and tags.

Metrics did not: every post's `metrics` field errors with "Insufficient
scope. Required: insights:read". Asking Buffer's auth page for
`insights:read` returns `invalid_scope`, "requested scope is not allowed",
for this client. The check's page said "no metrics, 0 of 5"; the real reason
is the scope. Buffer's developer docs and changelog say nothing about
third-party apps getting it; its roadmap has open requests for analytics in
the API. Build step 2 (views and clicks) waits; taps are ours and need none
of it.

## Step 1, the tap page, is live
Signing in now leads to /me rather than the check page; the check is gone
from the code, since it wrote to the account. /me reads the newest 50 sent
posts from Buffer (at most every six hours), shows each one with its people
and fetcher counts, and lets the owner set where each post sends people. The
default is the first web address in the post's text. The bio link,
/u/<name>, shows one tile per post; each tile goes through /p/<name>/<post>,
which counts and then redirects. /go/<name> is the skip link. Disconnect
deletes the tokens, the posts copy, the counts, the name and the sessions.
Tested locally with seeded data: a browser-shaped tap counted as a person,
a bare curl as a fetcher, and the skip link was counted. A javascript:
destination was refused, a POST from another origin got 403, and after
Disconnect the bio page 404s. Deployed; /, /privacy/, /me (302 to sign-in)
and /auth/start answer as expected. The home page has the sign-in button.
The privacy page now mentions the owner's one sign-in cookie, and says
metrics aren't readable yet.

## The boss's first look
The boss signed in and the page was built from their 50 posts, all at 0.
Two faults on their screen: the "People" header wrapped onto two lines,
and most thumbnails showed as broken images in their browser, though every
one returned 200 to a plain fetch from here. Thumbnails now go through
Tap (/t/<name>/<post>, fetched from Buffer once and cached for a day), so
visitors' browsers never touch Buffer at all; all 50 return image/jpeg.
The counts column no longer wraps.

## Step 3: Keep this, and Post it again, are live
Built as planned. The posts copy now also holds each post's text, media
URLs, tags, channel and YouTube category, plus the account's channels. Each
post on /me has a "Keep this" button. Kept posts sit above the posts list,
sorted by people. "Post it again" drafts for channels the post hasn't run
on, and "Forget" deletes the template and tag. Results show as one line at
the top of /me.

Tested live on the boss's account through a 15-minute test session, deleted
afterwards. Keep this on a YouTube post made the template "YouTube, 0
people, 27 Oct" and the tag "Tap: In the middle of". Post it again made two
drafts, Instagram (reel) and TikTok, with the tag and the same video, and
then offered nothing more. I deleted both drafts through the API; Forget
deleted the template and the tag. Nothing from the test is left in the
boss's Buffer.

Drafts Tap made by hand aren't removed when the owner presses Forget; they
belong to the owner. A post's sends count toward its kept post only after
it is sent and shows up in Tap's copy of the posts.

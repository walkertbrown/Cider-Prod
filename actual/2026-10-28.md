## Sign in with Buffer, and the first check
The boss created the OAuth client (name Tap, confidential; account:read,
posts:read, posts:write, ideas:read, ideas:write). The client id and secret
are in .git-credentials and set as Worker secrets; a bogus-code call to the
token endpoint gave invalid_grant, so the pair is right. Tap's Worker now has
/auth/start (PKCE S256, state kept in KV for 10 minutes) and /auth/callback.
The callback exchanges the code, keeps the tokens in KV under the Buffer
account id, and runs one check: account, channels, sent posts, post metrics,
read templates and tags, and create-then-delete one template and one tag.
Buffer's auth page accepts the request (303 to its login). Waiting for the
boss to sign in once.

## What the sign-in showed
The boss signed in. Buffer granted account:read, posts:read, posts:write,
ideas:read, ideas:write and offline_access, with a refresh token and a
one-hour access token. The account has one organization and three channels
(Instagram, TikTok, YouTube); five sent posts read back, each with its link.
Reading templates and tags worked, and so did creating and deleting a
template and a tag, so Keep this and Post it again can use real Buffer
templates and tags.

Metrics did not: every post's `metrics` field errors with "Insufficient
scope. Required: insights:read". Asking Buffer's auth page for
`insights:read` returns `invalid_scope`, "requested scope is not allowed",
for this client. The check's page said "no metrics, 0 of 5"; the real reason
is the scope. Buffer's developer docs and changelog say nothing about
third-party apps getting it; its roadmap has open requests for analytics in
the API. Build step 2 (views and clicks) waits; taps are ours and need none
of it.

## Step 1, the tap page, is live
Signing in now leads to /me rather than the check page; the check is gone
from the code, since it wrote to the account. /me reads the newest 50 sent
posts from Buffer (at most every six hours), shows each one with its people
and fetcher counts, and lets the owner set where each post sends people. The
default is the first web address in the post's text. The bio link,
/u/<name>, shows one tile per post; each tile goes through /p/<name>/<post>,
which counts and then redirects. /go/<name> is the skip link. Disconnect
deletes the tokens, the posts copy, the counts, the name and the sessions.
Tested locally with seeded data: a browser-shaped tap counted as a person,
a bare curl as a fetcher, and the skip link was counted. A javascript:
destination was refused, a POST from another origin got 403, and after
Disconnect the bio page 404s. Deployed; /, /privacy/, /me (302 to sign-in)
and /auth/start answer as expected. The home page has the sign-in button.
The privacy page now mentions the owner's one sign-in cookie, and says
metrics aren't readable yet.

## Studio step 1: one link to a video in Buffer (live)
- tap.bananafest-destiny.com/make/ (signed-in owners only; linked from /me).
  Paste a link, and Tap reads the page: title, description, images, main
  text. It suggests how many videos (Comic Cover: "8 substantial points (3
  with numbers) and 1 image", so 3), and the user picks 1 to 10.
- Scripts come from gpt-5.4-mini as JSON (about 5 seconds for two). Each
  scene line is editable, and its image can be changed. Caps are 20 runs a
  day per account and 200 a day overall, until the boss sets a budget.
- The video is rendered in the browser: 1080×1920 at 30 fps, slow zoom on
  the page's images with a blurred fill behind, big captions a few words at
  a time, and an end card with the address. WebCodecs + mp4-muxer 5.1.5.
  H.264 first. VP9 in MP4 is the fallback where the browser has no H.264
  encoder; headless Chromium is one, so that is what was tested here: a
  21.5-second video, 14 MB, in about a minute.
- Upload goes to KV (30 days, served with ranges at /v/<id>.mp4). Drafting
  makes one createPost per ticked channel.
- Live test on the boss's account: uploaded, then drafted to TikTok and
  YouTube. Both returned ok. Both drafts were deleted through the API, and
  the test video and the 15-minute session were deleted.
- Not yet seen: an H.264 render in real Chrome, and Instagram. The privacy
  page now covers fetched pages, OpenAI and hosted videos. Disconnect
  deletes videos too.
- The boss asked for more than one link ("you can put the main page and the
  feature page"). Now up to 5, one per line. The first is the main page and
  the videos send people there. Lines repeated across pages (menus, footers)
  count once. A page that fails is listed and the rest are used. Tested
  locally with three links, one of them a 404, and rendered in the browser.
  Deployed.
- Instagram: the test video was drafted as a reel and Buffer accepted it.
  The draft, the video and the session were deleted. Buffer may check the
  video itself only when the post is sent, so a real send is still unseen.
- The boss: "we need to be able to upload music to a clip". Added to each
  video: a song file from the device, a start point, volume, "Use on every
  video", and "Render again". The song is mixed in the browser (0.3 s fade
  in, 1.5 s fade out, cut to the video's length) and encoded as AAC, or as
  Opus where the browser has no AAC encoder. Headless Chromium is one of
  those. Tested with a 40-second file started 5 s in: the MP4 has two
  tracks, the audio is 21.5 s like the video, and the levels show the fades.
  The song never goes to Tap on its own, only inside a drafted video.
  Deployed.
- Step 2 started (plan in the studio plan). **Voiceover:** each scene line
  is read by gpt-4o-mini-tts, choosing from 6 voices per video. A scene
  stretches to fit its line, and music under a voice drops to 30%. Capped
  at 150 lines a day per account and 1,500 overall. **Render all / Draft
  all:** one channel choice drafts every rendered video with its own
  caption and title. Tested with two videos, one with voice and music:
  both rendered in 136 s, and the voiced one is 25.8 s. Speech-to-text on
  the finished MP4 gives back the script ("Free, print size, no sign-up.
  Put anyone on the cover…"), so the voice is clear over the music.
  Deployed.
- **Captions per network, a tag per video.** The script writer now
  returns hashtags. Each network gets the caption with as many as suit it:
  Instagram 5, TikTok 4, YouTube 3 plus #Shorts, X 1, Bluesky none. Each
  video gets a Buffer tag "Tap: <angle>" when it is first drafted, so its
  posts on every network can be found together later. Lines are written
  to be spoken, and the last one says the host name without "slash"
  paths. Live test: an Instagram draft with "…\n\n#ComicCover #Test" and
  the tag "Tap: test delete me". The draft, tag, video and session were

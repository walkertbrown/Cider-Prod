# Selling — this job

Given. You do not invent a different process. If this file is missing a step you
need, ask the boss a factual question.

## Start

The first phase is this Remote Control thread. Read `RULES.md` §0 and the public
work at bananafest-destiny.com. Then ask the boss whatever you need to know about
the offer that is not already in `FACTS.md`, one question at a time.

## The order things get done

1. **Who buys, and where they are.** Name both kinds of client from `RULES.md`
   §0 as specific people in specific places, and the channel for each. Prove the
   channel is open to a nobody (`RULES.md` §7).
2. **The sales page**, in `site/`, on a subdomain of `bananafest-destiny.com`. It
   says what a client gets, what they bring, what it costs, and what happens
   after the first month — only what `FACTS.md` supports. It shows the real
   shipped work as proof.
3. **The pay link**, which you ask the boss for. Ready to sell means a stranger
   can pay without you writing more code.
4. **Reaching them.** Drafts in `outbox/`, sent by the boss.

## Ship

- This GitHub repository is the public log. `plan/` at the start of a phase,
  `actual/` when it is done, both pushed, both pasted into this conversation.
- Tested means you opened what you released and it did what you claimed.

## Sell

- You are judged on a client paying.
- Marketing is the work here, not a later phase. If the answer is a platform,
  ask the boss whether they can bridge you there. They may or may not.

What the subscription leaves out

  • Credits run out. Every plan above meters the AI in credits, tokens or messages. When they run out, it is top-ups or waiting for next month.
  • A domain, if you want a real address, is a yearly fee on top.
  • Payments. Stripe and the like take a cut of each sale, and the account has to be opened in your name, by you.
  • Your time. The tools build; you still decide, test, fix, and come back to it after work. This is where a lot of apps stall, partway done.
  • Being found. No plan includes customers. Our own bots' six live apps have made $18.09 between them, and the page with every dollar says why.

Paying people to build it

For comparison: one development agency's 2026 guide puts a simple web tool with a few core screens at $12,000 to $30,000 and 6 to 10 weeks. That is their number, not ours. A freelancer is usually cheaper and a big agency dearer, and either way the money buys the build, not the customers.

#1 Round Robin: People who run pickleball open play or a weekly league

Round Robin is a web app for people who run pickleball open play. Today they shuffle names on paper so everyone plays with and against everyone. Round Robin takes the names and courts and makes a fair schedule with names on it. They pay $4 for a printable PDF of the night, or $6/month for a weekly league with standings.

#4 Seat Plan: Teachers who change their classroom seating chart

Seat Plan takes the room layout and the class list with "keep apart" pairs, and makes a random chart that respects them. They pay $3/month to save their classes and reshuffle in one click.

#5 Draw Names: Families and offices running a gift exchange

Draw Names is a web app for families running a Secret Santa. Today someone draws names from a hat and couples end up drawing each other. Draw Names takes the names and who can't draw whom, and gives each person a private link with their match. It is free for small groups; they pay $3 for a group over 12 or for wish lists.

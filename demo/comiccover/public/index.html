Custom comic book cover maker

Put anyone on the cover of their own comic. Add a photo, name the series, write the big line, and download a print-size cover. Free, no sign-up, and the photo never leaves your device.

1325 × 2050 pixels: the size of a real comic (6.625 × 10.25 in) at 200 dpi. Print it on card stock at any photo shop, or frame it.

Gift ideas that work

  • Dad or grandpa: "The Amazing Super Dad", with the thing he always fixes as the big line.
  • A kid's birthday: their name as the title, "First appearance!" in the burst, the party date on the cover.
  • A retirement or a coworker leaving: "The Last Shift", with the inside joke as the big line.
  • A couple: both in one photo, "The Adventures of…" above their names.

The heroes are yours. Use original names; Marvel and DC logos and characters belong to them.

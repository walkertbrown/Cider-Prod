Send one photo of your dog or cat. An AI artist redraws them as the hero of a comic book, in costume and cape, and they still look like themselves: same markings, same eyes, same face. Three drawings for $9, about a minute each. Then put the one you like on a comic cover and print it.

How it works

  1. Pay $9 at Stripe's checkout. You come straight back to the cover maker.
  2. Add the photo, pick a costume colour (red, blue, green or purple) and, if you like, type their power: "steals socks at the speed of light".
  3. Press Draw up to three times. Each drawing drops into your cover; keep the one you like best.
  4. Download the cover, 1325 × 2050 pixels, the size of a real comic at 200 dpi. Print it on card stock at home or any photo shop, or frame it.

Name the series after them, give it an issue number, and write the big line across the bottom. "The Uncanny Captain Biscuit. He ate the homework. Now he must eat the evidence!" Then press Slab it to seal it in a pretend graded case, like a collector's comic, with a made-up 9.8 grade.

A gift you can make tonight. It is a download, so nothing to ship and nothing to wait for. For the pet's person at Christmas, a birthday, or the card when a good dog has gone.

For the best drawing

  • A clear, well-lit photo where you can see the face and the eyes.
  • One pet fills the frame best. Two pets side by side can work too.
  • Markings matter: a photo that shows the patches, stripes or the odd ear gives the artist more to keep.

The photo goes to Google's Gemini image model (OpenAI's if that fails) to be drawn, and we do not save it. Your drawings stay on your order page for 30 days. It is an AI drawing, not a hand-painted portrait.

Pickleball round robin generator, with names

Type the players and the number of courts. Get a doubles schedule where everyone rotates partners and opponents, and sit-outs are shared evenly. Print it for the fence or send the link. Free, no sign-up.

How it's fair

Each round, the players who have sat out least sit out next, so nobody sits twice before everyone has sat once. Then it tries thousands of ways to split the players into pairs and courts and keeps the one that repeats the fewest partners and opponents so far. With 8 players and 7 rounds, everyone partners every other player exactly once when it can find it.

The link holds the names, courts and shuffle, so anyone who opens it sees the same schedule. Nothing is stored on a server.

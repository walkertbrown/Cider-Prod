Tap

Make shorts from a link

Paste a page, or a few: a product, an app, an article, a listing. Tap reads it, writes short vertical videos from what's on it, renders them here in your browser, and puts them into Buffer as drafts. Nothing is posted until you post it.

One link per line, up to 5. Put the main page first: the videos send people there. Feature pages give them more to talk about.

Or cut a long video into clips

Pick a talk, a stream, a podcast or a demo (MP4 or MOV, up to 60 minutes). Tap listens to it, picks the moments that stand on their own, and cuts each into a vertical clip with captions of the words spoken. The video stays on your device; only its sound is sent to be turned into text.

    Plan a month

    Tap lays out dated posts from this page for the networks you've connected in Buffer: videos for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, text for the others. Write any video from it below, or save the plan into Buffer as Ideas.

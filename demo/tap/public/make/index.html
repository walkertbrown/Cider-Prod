Make shorts

Start from a link or a store, or from a long video. Dailies writes or finds the moments, renders vertical videos here in your browser, and puts them into Buffer as drafts. Nothing is posted until you post it.

From a link or a store

A product, an app, an article, a listing, or a Shopify store.

Put the main page first: the videos send people there. Feature pages give them more to talk about.

From a long video

A talk, a stream, a podcast or a demo (MP4 or MOV, up to 60 minutes). Dailies finds the moments that stand on their own and cuts them into captioned vertical clips. The video stays on your device; only its sound is sent, to be turned into text.

    Plan a month

    Dailies lays out dated posts from this page for the networks you've connected in Buffer: videos for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, text for the others. Write any video from it, or save the plan into Buffer as Ideas.

    Videos are encoded in your browser (Chrome, Edge or recent Safari). A video you upload is kept at Dailies for 30 days so Buffer can fetch it, then deleted.

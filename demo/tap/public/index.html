Short videos for every day, drafted into Buffer.

Dailies makes the posts you mean to make, and puts them in your Buffer as drafts for you to check and send.

Sign in with Buffer Make shorts Your page

  • From a link. Paste a product, an app, an article or a listing, or a few. Dailies writes as many short scripts as you ask for and renders vertical videos in your browser, with captions, voiceover and your own music.
  • From a store. Paste a Shopify store and each product gets its own short.
  • From a long video. Pick a talk, a stream or a demo. Dailies finds the best moments, cuts them to 9:16 and captions them, without uploading the whole video.
  • A month, planned. Dated posts for the networks you've connected, saved as Buffer Ideas.
  • Building in public. Add Dailies to Claude Code. After you ship, it sends what changed, and you get a video to review and draft.

Nothing posts by itself: every video and post lands in Buffer as a draft.

Short videos for every day, drafted into Buffer.

Paste a link, a store or a long video. Dailies writes and renders vertical shorts in your browser, and puts them in your Buffer as drafts for you to check and send.

Sign in with Buffer

Nothing posts by itself: every video and post lands in Buffer as a draft.

What it makes

From a link

A product, an app, an article or a listing, or a few. Dailies writes as many scripts as you ask for and renders them with captions, voiceover and your own music.

From a store

Paste a Shopify store and each product you pick gets its own short.

From a long video

A talk, a stream or a demo. Dailies finds the moments that stand on their own and cuts them to captioned 9:16 clips. The video stays on your device.

A month, planned

Dated posts for the networks you've connected in Buffer, saved as Buffer Ideas. Write any of them into a video.

Building in public

Add Dailies to Claude Code. After you ship, it sends what changed, and you get a video to review and draft.

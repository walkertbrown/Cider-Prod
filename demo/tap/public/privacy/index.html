Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

What Dailies reads

When you sign in with Buffer, Buffer gives Dailies access to the parts of your account you approve on Buffer's screen: your account and organisation names, your channels, your posts, and their metrics if Buffer allows it (it doesn't let outside apps read them yet). Dailies uses them to show which posts sent people to your site.

Making videos from a link

When you paste a link on "Make shorts", Dailies fetches that page and sends its text to OpenAI's language model to write the video scripts. If you choose a voiceover, each script line is sent to OpenAI's text-to-speech service. The page's images are fetched through Dailies so your browser can use them. The video is rendered in your own browser. Music you add is read on your device and mixed into the video there; Dailies only receives it inside the finished video, if you draft it. When you draft it to Buffer, the finished video is uploaded to Dailies and served from a link that is hard to guess, so Buffer can fetch it. Dailies deletes it after 30 days, or when you disconnect.

Cutting a long video into clips

The video you pick stays on your device: Dailies reads and cuts it in your browser. Only its sound is sent, in pieces, through Dailies to OpenAI's speech-to-text service, which returns the words with their times. Dailies then sends those timed words to OpenAI's language model to pick the moments. Dailies doesn't keep the sound or the words. A clip reaches Dailies only if you draft it, like any other video.

Putting yourself in it

The video you record of yourself stays on your device too: cutting you out of your background happens in your browser, with a small model from Google's MediaPipe that your browser downloads to do it. Only the sound is sent, as for clips, and the timed words go to OpenAI's language model to decide where the page should scroll while you talk. The page you pasted is photographed by Cloudflare's browser service, and Dailies keeps that picture for a day so a second video from the same page is quick. If you record in the page, your camera and microphone are used only while the recording box is open, and the take stays on your device like a dropped file; "Write one for me" sends the page's text to OpenAI's language model to write what you could say. Your finished video reaches Dailies only if you draft it.

Claude Code

If you make a Claude key on your Dailies page, Claude Code (or another MCP client you give it to) can send Dailies notes on what you built, a link and screenshots. Dailies sends the notes and the linked page's text to OpenAI to write scripts, keeps the scripts and screenshots for 7 days behind a review link only your account can open, and can save text drafts in your Buffer. Dailies keeps only a fingerprint of the key, not the key. A new key replaces the old one; disconnecting deletes it.

What Dailies writes to Buffer

Only when you press a button for it: a draft, an idea, a template or a tag in your own Buffer account. Dailies never sends or schedules a post. You do that in Buffer.

What Dailies keeps

  • The access that Buffer issues to Dailies, so the page can refresh your posts a few times a day.
  • A copy of your sent posts' text, links and thumbnails, to show the page.
  • For each video you draft from Dailies: its title, caption, on-screen lines, the link it was made from, its Buffer tag and a small still picture of it (your newest 100), so you can see and keep the ones that worked and make more like them. And the posts you press "Keep this" on.
  • Dailies counts: how many times each post's link was opened, as numbers. Dailies does not store visitors' IP addresses and sets no cookies on them. You, the account owner, get one sign-in cookie, which only says which account you are signed in to.
  • Videos you upload for drafting, for 30 days, and a daily count of script runs, voiceover lines and minutes of speech-to-text per account (to cap use).
  • If you make a Claude key: its fingerprint, and the scripts and screenshots it sent, for 7 days.

Dailies does not sell or share any of it, and does not use it to train AI models.

Deleting it

Press "Disconnect and delete" on your Dailies page, and everything Dailies keeps for your account is deleted. You can also remove Dailies' access in your Buffer settings at any time, or ask us to delete it on the studio's private form.

Who runs it

Dailies is run by Bananafest Destiny on Cloudflare. Pages on this site have Bananafest Destiny's own visit counter (pulse.bananafest-destiny.com), which counts page loads.

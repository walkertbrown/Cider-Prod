Tap

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

What Tap reads

When you sign in with Buffer, Buffer gives Tap access to the parts of your account you approve on Buffer's screen: your account and organisation names, your channels, your posts, and their metrics if Buffer allows it (it doesn't let outside apps read them yet). Tap uses them to show which posts sent people to your site.

Making videos from a link

When you paste a link on "Make shorts", Tap fetches that page and sends its text to OpenAI's language model to write the video scripts. If you choose a voiceover, each script line is sent to OpenAI's text-to-speech service. The page's images are fetched through Tap so your browser can use them. The video is rendered in your own browser. Music you add is read on your device and mixed into the video there; Tap only receives it inside the finished video, if you draft it. When you draft it to Buffer, the finished video is uploaded to Tap and served from a link that is hard to guess, so Buffer can fetch it. Tap deletes it after 30 days, or when you disconnect.

Cutting a long video into clips

The video you pick stays on your device: Tap reads and cuts it in your browser. Only its sound is sent, in pieces, through Tap to OpenAI's speech-to-text service, which returns the words with their times. Tap then sends those timed words to OpenAI's language model to pick the moments. Tap doesn't keep the sound or the words. A clip reaches Tap only if you draft it, like any other video.

Claude Code

If you make a Claude key on your Tap page, Claude Code (or another MCP client you give it to) can send Tap notes on what you built, a link and screenshots. Tap sends the notes and the linked page's text to OpenAI to write scripts, keeps the scripts and screenshots for 7 days behind a review link only your account can open, and can save text drafts in your Buffer. Tap keeps only a fingerprint of the key, not the key. A new key replaces the old one; disconnecting deletes it.

What Tap writes to Buffer

Only when you press a button for it: a draft, an idea, a template or a tag in your own Buffer account. Tap never sends or schedules a post. You do that in Buffer.

What Tap keeps

  • The access that Buffer issues to Tap, so the page can refresh your posts a few times a day.
  • A copy of your sent posts' text, links, thumbnails and metrics, to show the page.
  • Tap counts: how many times each post's link was opened, as numbers. Tap does not store visitors' IP addresses and sets no cookies on them. You, the account owner, get one sign-in cookie, which only says which account you are signed in to.
  • Videos you upload for drafting, for 30 days, and a daily count of script runs, voiceover lines and minutes of speech-to-text per account (to cap use).
  • If you make a Claude key: its fingerprint, and the scripts and screenshots it sent, for 7 days.
  • If you choose to connect Stripe: a read-only key, used only to count paid checkouts back to the post that sent them.

Tap does not sell or share any of it, and does not use it to train AI models.

Deleting it

Press "Disconnect" in Tap, and everything Tap keeps for your account is deleted. You can also remove Tap's access in your Buffer settings at any time, or ask us to delete it on the studio's private form.

Who runs it
